## Semantic-license dimensions
A proposed diversified family may be ACCEPTED only when all required dimensions for that family are positively supported and no contradiction is detected.

Common dimensions:
1. obligation kind/function compatibility;
2. required information movement;
3. relationship target;
4. physical affordance;
5. causal preconditions / identifiable prior move where required;
6. unresolved/deferred preservation;
7. unsupported-event / unsupported-state invention risk;
8. explicit negative or anti-resolution language;
9. whether the family would prematurely settle, reinterpret, or redirect the obligation.

Absence of positive support is not support.

Ambiguity resolves to ABSTAIN.

## Family-specific minimum licenses
### MISINTERPRETATION
Require an explicit existing false-belief / mistaken-reading / mistaken-identity state that the obligation itself must dramatize.
A mere sensor error, uncertainty, need to verify evidence, or wording that says not to misread / not to conclude is insufficient.

### COST_BEARING_CHOICE
Require an actual choice between alternatives plus an obligation-level cost/sacrifice that must be borne by the choosing actor.
A cost mentioned only as obstacle/background is insufficient.

### PHYSICAL_RISK_FAILURE
Require the physical risk/failure itself to be the causal transaction required by the obligation.
Physical danger in the setting or obstacle is insufficient for a relationship/information transaction.

### NEGOTIATION_EXCHANGE
Require an actual conditional exchange, bargain, concession, resource/access/information trade or reciprocal term.
Multiple owners alone are insufficient.

### RELATIONSHIP_BOUNDARY_CHANGE
Require a relationship target or relationship delta involving access, distance, trust, contact, cooperation or refusal.

### WITHHELD_INFORMATION_MOVE
Require an intentional information-withholding action by an actor.
Missing, unavailable or uncertain information alone is insufficient.

### DISCOVERY
Require that the obligation calls for revealing or establishing a new fact from evidence.
Simple verification of a known proposition may abstain to PROBE_INFORMATION when discovery would invent a new fact.

### SOCIAL_INSTITUTIONAL_PRESSURE
Require a group/institutional rule, approval, allocation or authority mechanism that directly constrains the action.

### PARTIAL_CONSEQUENCE
Require a supported prior event/dependency whose consequence is due to become partially concrete now.

### COUNTERMOVE
Require an identifiable prior opposing move or controlling action to answer.

### FAILED_ATTEMPT
Require a direct attempt whose failure is both causally supported and compatible with the obligation remaining unresolved until RESOLVE.
Do not invent failure when the obligation requires immediate successful execution.

### PRESSURE_ESCALATION
Require an existing pressure trajectory that must become more constraining.
Obstacle presence alone is insufficient.
