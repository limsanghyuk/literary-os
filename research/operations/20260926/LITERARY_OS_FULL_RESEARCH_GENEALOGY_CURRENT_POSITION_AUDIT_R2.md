## 11. Container / reproducibility operating lessons

Repeated ClientError, TransportTimeout, upload truncation and mount/materialization failures were separated from scientific failure.

Canonical recovery rule:
STOP -> RUNTIME_TRANSPORT_HOLD -> MINIMAL_HEALTH_CHECK -> LAST_ATOMIC_CHECKPOINT_VERIFY -> RESUME_ONLY_FAILED_STEP.

Operational learning:
- one large archive / one step at a time;
- use streaming I/O;
- use /tmp for temporary rejoin/extraction;
- verify size -> SHA -> central directory -> CRC before mutation;
- distinguish page-cache pressure from actual OOM using memory.events;
- do not treat visible file presence as byte authority;
- do not treat raw-byte materialization denial as corruption;
- preserve hidden mapping/frozen-input custody or HOLD the experiment;
- nested historical CURRENT files cannot override external current Manifest/Trust Root/Handoff;
- same-name/different-hash objects are quarantined rather than silently merged.

These rules are now part of the project’s scientific reproducibility discipline, not merely IT troubleshooting.

## 12. Craft constraints learned through the research

Current surface doctrine:
- dialogue should not carry explanatory emotion/exposition when action/direction can carry it;
- direction may describe location, time, scene function, action, visible emotion and performance handling;
- important changes should be made visible through action, hands, props, position changes, pauses, failed actions, silence and facial-expression change;
- no post-action explanatory restatement after the action already communicates the meaning;
- broadcast episode minimum is now >=40,000 characters for the current production-scale qualification line;
- no upper cap;
- 9-10 sequences and 45-50 scenes are minimum-scale conventions, not rigid constants.
