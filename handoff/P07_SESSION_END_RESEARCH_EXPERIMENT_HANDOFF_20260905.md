## F. Ensemble / Social Ecology is a first-class causal architecture
Ensemble is not 'many characters appear'. It is distributed causal ownership.

A strong drama architecture must represent at minimum:
- protagonist individual/group,
- opposing individual/group,
- family/work/school/organization/friend networks,
- secondary and tertiary characters with independent wants,
- group membership and cross-group relationships,
- information/resource ownership,
- event and plot-axis ownership,
- relationship/thread obligations,
- episode-level due/defer distribution.

Historical R41/P01 evidence supported Main-only < Multi-axis < Ensemble, and the R75-R77 lineage specifically targeted social ecology/event ownership and protagonist-bias reduction. Therefore P07 cannot close with an 'ENSEMBLE_ECOLOGY_PLAN' label alone; the runtime must prove actual consumption and downstream behavioral effect.
