## 1. Why this session matters / 이번 세션 연구의 위치

The project had already built a substantial lower and middle narrative stack: Narrative State, Planning, Realization, Repair, renderer/craft work, causal replay, database/retrieval and state-carry mechanisms. This I4K session deliberately returned to the **upper narrative layer**.

The objective was no longer a one-shot plot generator that injects a predesigned incident. The research question became:

> Can Literary OS maintain a living ecology in which character state, relationships, social/institutional forces, environment/chance, world state and future obligations continuously interact, produce candidate events, propagate consequences through other actors, and allow a higher-order selector/showrunner process to decide which events enter the work?

Accepted target progression:

1. `Narrative State / Planning / Realization / Repair OS`
2. **`Narrative Event Ecology Engine`**
3. **`Narrative Showrunner Operating System`**

The long-range analogy is not a single author receiving a plot prompt. It is closer to a **writers-room / showrunner ecology**: multiple characters and world forces remain active, create pressures, transfer information/cost/authority, generate future obligations, and compete for narrative adoption.

This upper-layer direction is now a **promoted Research Architecture**, but that is distinct from Active Engine, Production or Formal promotion.

## 3. I4K program architecture / I4K 연구 구조

The I4K track was designed as:

- **K1 World Reality Retrieval** — mine real-world mechanisms, not plots.
- **K2 Event Primitive Extractor** — abstract mechanisms such as deadlines, approval chains, unavailable liquidity, service remedies, incident tradeoffs and infrastructure dependencies.
- **K3 Perspective Expander** — expose the same mechanism to multiple actors/positions.
- **K4 Character/Relationship Event Generator** — generate events rooted in internal character and relationship state.
- **K5 Social/Institutional Event Generator** — generate events from institutional/social rules and pressures.
- **K6 Environmental/Chance Event Generator** — generate environmental/chance pressures without deus-ex-machina resolution.
- **K7 Event Ecology Simulator** — propagate events, burdens and decisions through the ensemble.
- **K8 Narrative Event Selector** — decide which generated event candidates should enter the work.
- **K9 Responsible-Ancestor Integration** — connect lower and upper planning through bidirectional/ancestor repair.

Required event origin classes:

- `INTERNAL_CHARACTER_EVENT`
- `RELATIONSHIP_EVENT`
- `SOCIAL_INSTITUTIONAL_EVENT`
- `ENVIRONMENTAL_CHANCE_EVENT`

The key conceptual shift is from **event injection** to **event ecology**.

## 5. Important broadcast-script format correction / 방송대본 지문 형식 교정

During post-I4K-5 diagnosis, the discussion risked overgeneralizing a real problem: a long scene-opening direction block was being treated as suspicious merely because it appeared before dialogue or occupied many characters.

That interpretation was corrected **before fresh repair outputs**.

Three surface categories must be kept separate:

### A. Scene-opening situation direction / 씬 시작 상황지문 — PROTECTED

May describe:

- location and time;
- atmosphere and environment;
- ongoing action/flow;
- performer emotional/physical state;
- blocking;
- props;
- production/performance context needed to understand the scene.

It may be detailed. **Length or placement before dialogue is not a defect by itself.**

### B. Dialogue-adjacent performer cue / 대사 인접 배우 지시 — ALLOWED

A concise emotional, behavioral or delivery cue can be placed close to the affected line, including parenthetical style `(…)` or a short local action line. Parenthetical form is valid but not mandatory for every beat.

### C. Planning/meta explanation / 계획·메타 설명문 — REPAIR TARGET

Prohibited at screenplay surface when not needed for production/performance understanding:

- `Event Ecology`, `CHxx`, `SQxx`, `thread`, `engine`, `planner`, Control/Treatment labels;
- copied sequence-purpose paragraphs;
- architecture rationale explaining why a scene exists;
- generic identical direction templates reused mechanically;
- dialogue that explains narrative architecture rather than acting dramatically.

The correct repair doctrine is therefore:

> **Do not make stage direction shorter merely to look cleaner. Keep rich legitimate stage direction; remove plan/meta leakage and mechanical repetition.**

Also, whole-episode length is now measured on **metadata-excluded screenplay body**, avoiding the earlier header-count ambiguity.
