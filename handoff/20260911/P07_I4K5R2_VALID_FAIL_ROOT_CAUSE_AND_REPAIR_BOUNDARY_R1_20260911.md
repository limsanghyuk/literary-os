## Surface defects still visible despite hygiene repair
1. **Abstract scene-tail interpretation remains too frequent.** A number of scenes end by verbally summarizing what the action means (for example, that responsibility has become distributed or a temporary rule has become future governance) after the action has already shown it. These lines are semantically correct but add little playability.
2. **Procedural nouns still carry too much dramatic load.** Boards, records, roles, rules, responsibility, handoff, and conditions recur as explicit dialogue/stage concepts. They support causal clarity but can make the broadcast surface feel designed rather than lived.
3. **Physical performance specificity is uneven.** Strong scenes bind a decision shift to an observable behavior, prop, blocking change, mishearing, refusal, interruption, gaze, or handoff. Weaker scenes state the decision correctly but do not give the actor/director an equally distinctive physical beat.
4. **Location-specific realization is present but not consistently causal.** Rain, monitors, whiteboards, maps, receipts, doors, ramps and radios are good concrete elements; however, some are atmosphere or bookkeeping rather than the mechanism that forces the choice.
5. **Character voice is improved but can still converge around governance vocabulary.** The same abstract terms can migrate across characters, reducing non-generic differentiation even when the underlying decision ownership is correct.

## Next repair boundary
The next experiment must change only the lower realization layer. Proposed intervention:

### Performance-Specific Surface Binding (PSSB)
- Bind each important scene-turn to at least one actor-visible or camera-legible physical consequence already licensed by the scene semantics.
- Prefer action, prop use, blocking, timing, interruption, silence, failed action, bodily effort, or spatial change over explanatory scene-tail interpretation.
- Remove or convert abstract post-action explanatory tails when the visible action already carries the meaning.
- Compress procedural exposition into character-specific exchange and behavior without deleting required semantic facts.
- Increase character-specific rhythm and local vocabulary without adding new backstory or semantic events.
- Make location/prop details causal where available rather than adding decorative detail.

This is a renderer/surface intervention only. It may not add events, scenes, sequences, decision owners, future-thread owners, or causal mechanisms absent from the shared upstream plan.
