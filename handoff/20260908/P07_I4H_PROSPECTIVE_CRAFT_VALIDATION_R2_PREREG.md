## Why this unit exists
I4F showed that broad mode application over-intervenes: NONE was blind-best on 10/12 prospective scenes. I4G then showed that reducing explanatory dialogue can still damage character voice, humor, lived rhythm, and ensemble social texture. The prior I4H architecture pretest therefore introduced a three-stage doctrine:

1. `INTERVENE?` vs `ABSTAIN` with a strong abstention prior;
2. if intervention is eligible, `OUTPUT RELIABLE?` vs `REJECT`;
3. only after reliability acceptance choose `LOW` vs `STANDARD` intensity.

The architecture pretest passed mechanically, but the prior craft diagnostic used only two previously inspected scenes and same-session evaluation. This R2 unit tests fresh synthetic scene material with generation completed before evaluation.

## Hypothesis
A conservative selector that abstains on socially textured scenes and applies targeted LOW/STANDARD intervention only on scenes with strong procedural/reveal/kinetic pressure will preserve voice/humor/social rhythm while retaining I4D-style gains in action, information control, and scene pressure.

## Research questions
1. Is the frozen selected arm best/co-best on most fresh synthetic scenes?
2. Does explicit abstention protect family, relationship, ensemble, community, and comedy scenes from harmful compression?
3. On intervention-eligible scenes, does LOW/STANDARD improve or preserve craft versus Control without violating semantic fidelity?
4. Does the selected intervention avoid the malformed-Korean / over-procedural / voice-flattening failure classes observed in I4F/I4G?
