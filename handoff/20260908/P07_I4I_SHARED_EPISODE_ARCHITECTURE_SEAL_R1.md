## EpisodeSynopsisPlan — 《야간편성》 synthetic episode

A local late-night radio team prepares a live neighborhood special about redevelopment. Sponsor liaison 민성재 asks programming director 최태식 to remove a resident segment that could make the sponsor uncomfortable. The request is not treated as proof of wrongdoing; the team must separate editorial pressure, genuine verification needs, and rumor.

Reporter 고은채 discovers that one dramatic claim circulating among residents cannot be verified, while a less sensational but documentable displacement issue can. Veteran host 정미라 insists that the broadcast should become more precise rather than simply louder. Junior writer 이준호 keeps the team socially connected through humor but also makes mistakes under time pressure. Snack-bar owner 김복자 becomes an important community voice without functioning as an exposition machine.

At the same time, a routing fault threatens the remote live connection. PD 한서윤 and sound engineer 박도진 must work together under pressure. Their unfinished personal trust is moved through professional action: 서윤 must allow 도진 to override one of her habitual control choices; 도진 must stop turning every disagreement into a joke. They do not confess or reconcile romantically.

The episode turn is not heroic defiance. The team airs only what it can verify, refuses to delete verified community voices merely to satisfy sponsor discomfort, and keeps the broadcast physically on air despite the technical fault. 최태식 accepts institutional responsibility for that editorial choice rather than leaving 서윤 alone to absorb it.

Terminal state:
- broadcast completes;
- verified resident material airs;
- unverified rumor is withheld;
- sponsor relationship remains tense but unresolved;
- station is not magically safe;
- 서윤/도진 trust reopens through work, no confession;
- ensemble remains active for subsequent episodes.

## SequencePlan

### SQ01 — THE REQUEST ARRIVES (5 scenes)
Goal: establish live-special preparation, ensemble tone, and sponsor request without prematurely moralizing it.
Turn: 최태식 tells 서윤 the sponsor wants one resident segment removed; he frames it as a request, not an order.

- S01 newsroom warm-up: 준호 reads an overdramatic listener intro; 미라 punctures it with humor. Speakers 미라/준호/서윤.
- S02 은채 arrives with verification notes; 서윤 makes clear that strong claims need records, not just good tape. Speakers 서윤/은채.
- S03 도진 tests remote line; intermittent audio click appears but is not yet a failure. Speakers 도진/준호.
- S04 태식 asks 서윤 into corridor; sponsor discomfort is introduced. Speakers 태식/서윤.
- S05 서윤 returns without announcing a crisis; she tells team the resident segment is now verification priority. Speakers 서윤/미라/은채/준호.

### SQ02 — VERIFY BEFORE DEFY (5 scenes)
Goal: distinguish editorial independence from reflexive opposition.
Turn: one resident allegation is marked unverified; another quieter displacement pattern remains supportable.

- S06 은채 and 준호 compare notes; 준호 wants the strongest quote, 은채 asks what can be proven. Speakers 은채/준호.
- S07 복자 snack-bar call test; her humor reveals lived disruption without making legal claims. Speakers 복자/미라/준호.
- S08 서윤 asks 은채 to label each claim VERIFIED / CONTEXT / HOLD. Speakers 서윤/은채.
- S09 미라 objects to removing all emotion just because one claim is uncertain. Speakers 미라/서윤.
- S10 은채 isolates one unverified rumor and one documentable relocation fact. Speakers 은채/서윤/미라.

### SQ03 — THE ROUTING FAULT (5 scenes)
Goal: introduce technical pressure as a real second plot, not decorative ticking clock.
Turn: 도진 proves the remote line can fail under load and needs a backup path before air.

- S11 도진 reproduces click under simultaneous remote channels. Speakers 도진/준호.
- S12 서윤 initially wants to keep schedule and fix after rehearsal; 도진 refuses false confidence. Speakers 서윤/도진.
- S13 comic cable hunt with 준호; he labels identical adapters badly. Speakers 준호/도진.
- S14 태식 asks whether delay is necessary; 도진 gives bounded risk, not melodrama. Speakers 태식/도진/서윤.
- S15 서윤 authorizes backup routing rehearsal, giving 도진 operational lead for the fault. Speakers 서윤/도진.

### SQ04 — COMMUNITY IS NOT A SOUND BITE (5 scenes)
Goal: give residents ensemble texture and test the team's tendency to compress them into a single message.
Turn: 복자 rejects a prewritten emotional line and gives a less polished but more truthful account.

- S16 field producer call with 복자; she jokes about being coached. Speakers 복자/준호.
- S17 복자 explains a practical change in customer traffic through daily details, not policy lecture. Speakers 복자/미라.
- S18 준호 offers a cleaner line; 복자 says she would never say it. Speakers 준호/복자.
- S19 미라 keeps 복자의 awkward original phrasing because it carries voice. Speakers 미라/서윤/준호.
- S20 서윤 marks the segment usable with factual caveat attached elsewhere, not inside 복자's mouth. Speakers 서윤/은채.

### SQ05 — SPONSOR ROOM (5 scenes)
Goal: play status negotiation through language, not villain exposition.
Turn: 태식 refuses to promise deletion but agrees to hear specific factual concerns.

- S21 민성재 arrives at station, polite and pressed for time. Speakers 성재/태식.
- S22 성재 says sponsor does not want to be linked to an unverified accusation. Speakers 성재/태식/서윤.
- S23 서윤 separates the held rumor from the verified resident segment. Speakers 서윤/성재.
- S24 성재 asks why the verified segment still needs to air; 태식 does not answer for 서윤. Speakers 성재/태식/서윤.
- S25 태식 says factual correction is negotiable, discomfort is not an editorial category. Speakers 태식/성재.

### SQ06 — WORK TRUST (5 scenes)
Goal: move 서윤/도진 relationship through task dependence while preserving dry humor.
Turn: 서윤 allows 도진 to rewrite the routing order; 도진 stops using a joke to avoid explaining why the old order is unsafe.

- S26 도진 hands 서윤 revised routing sheet; she sees her original order crossed out. Speakers 도진/서윤.
- S27 서윤 asks why he changed it; 도진 starts with joke, she waits. Speakers 도진/서윤.
- S28 도진 explains one concrete failure chain; no emotional confession. Speakers 도진/서윤.
- S29 서윤 signs the revised order and tells him the live call is his at failure point. Speakers 서윤/도진.
- S30 quiet equipment-room beat; 도진 says '이번엔 안 뺏겠습니다' about control, carrying double meaning without resolving it. Speakers 도진/서윤.

### SQ07 — THE LESS DRAMATIC TRUTH (5 scenes)
Goal: make verification produce a better story rather than merely remove material.
Turn: the team drops the sensational rumor and finds a verifiable resident-impact sequence strong enough to carry the segment.

- S31 은채 receives confirming document for relocation timing. Speakers 은채/준호.
- S32 준호 is disappointed the 'bigger' claim is unusable; 은채 challenges his scale instinct. Speakers 은채/준호.
- S33 미라 listens to two cuts and chooses the less dramatic but accountable one. Speakers 미라/은채.
- S34 서윤 restructures the segment order around verified voices. Speakers 서윤/미라/준호.
- S35 태식 is told the held rumor will not air; he accepts without using that as reason to delete the rest. Speakers 태식/서윤.

### SQ08 — PRE-AIR FAILURE (5 scenes)
Goal: converge editorial and technical pressure without mixing their causality.
Turn: primary remote route fails during final rehearsal; backup must be activated under time pressure.

- S36 countdown begins; 준호 discovers a cue-sheet version mismatch. Speakers 준호/미라.
- S37 remote line drops under load. Speakers 도진/서윤/준호.
- S38 도진 calls for backup sequence; 서윤 must choose whether to delay or trust the rehearsed path. Speakers 도진/서윤.
- S39 서윤 authorizes backup; ensemble executes distinct tasks. Speakers 서윤/도진/준호/미라.
- S40 backup audio arrives imperfect but stable; they choose to air with a shorter remote window. Speakers 도진/서윤/미라.

### SQ09 — LIVE, FIRST HALF (5 scenes)
Goal: prove the show can be alive rather than merely procedural.
Turn: 미라 uses live conversation to keep resident voice human while 서윤 silently removes an unverified follow-up from cue.

- S41 opening broadcast; 미라 introduces neighborhood without slogan. Speakers 미라.
- S42 복자 live segment begins with a joke about radio delay. Speakers 미라/복자.
- S43 복자 gives lived detail; 준호 signals one risky follow-up question. Speakers 복자/미라/준호(off-mic cue only for surface direction).
- S44 서윤 crosses the unverified follow-up from cue; 미라 sees and pivots without dead air. Speakers 서윤/미라.
- S45 verified relocation detail lands through 은채's short fact check, not a lecture. Speakers 미라/은채.

### SQ10 — PRESSURE DURING AIR (5 scenes)
Goal: force institutional choice while broadcast is already in motion.
Turn: 태식 explicitly owns the decision to keep verified resident material on air.

- S46 성재 calls 태식 during break, asking if remaining segment can be cut. Speakers 성재/태식.
- S47 태식 asks 서윤 whether any factual issue remains; she says no verified issue, only discomfort. Speakers 태식/서윤.
- S48 태식 tells 성재 the verified segment will continue and corrections remain open if specific. Speakers 태식/성재.
- S49 도진 detects rising noise on backup route; he and 서윤 coordinate a shorter handoff. Speakers 도진/서윤.
- S50 show returns live with no speech about 'standing up to pressure'; action carries the institutional choice. Speakers 미라/복자.

### SQ11 — AFTER AIR, NOTHING IS MAGICALLY FIXED (4 scenes)
Goal: land consequences, ensemble aftertaste, and relationship carry-forward without overclosure.
Turn: the team completes the broadcast, sponsor tension remains, and 서윤/도진 reopen trust through work.

- S51 broadcast ends; room stays quiet for one beat before junior staff reacts. Speakers 미라/준호/은채/서윤/도진.
- S52 성재 sends a terse request for follow-up meeting; 태식 says tomorrow, not tonight. Speakers 태식/서윤.
- S53 복자 calls again only to complain that 미라 cut off her best joke; comedy restores social scale. Speakers 복자/미라/준호.
- S54 equipment room exit: 서윤 returns 도진's marked routing sheet instead of discarding it; she asks him to keep the changes for next week. He answers practically. No confession. Speakers 서윤/도진.
