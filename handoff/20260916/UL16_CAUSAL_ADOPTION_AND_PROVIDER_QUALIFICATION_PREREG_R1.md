H5 — **Hierarchy preservation.** Independent blind reconstruction can recover the frozen Episode/Sequence/Scene architecture from final surface materially better than the legacy Control.

## 7. Stage B — A2 causal-adoption interventions

Use four intervention classes, each tested on multiple eligible episode states:

1. **Relationship intervention** — change power/trust/debt/allegiance state while holding unrelated facts constant.
2. **Information intervention** — change who knows/believes/possesses a critical fact.
3. **Event/precondition intervention** — remove or alter a causal prerequisite.
4. **Payoff/deadline intervention** — raise/lower urgency or horizon of a planted obligation.

When explicit social-ecology evidence is available, add:
5. **Social-group intervention** — change membership/coalition/organizational constraint.

For every intervention, freeze:
- base state hash;
- intervention diff;
- expected affected obligation family;
- unrelated sentinel fields;
- expected direction of architectural change, not exact wording/count.

Primary pass logic:
- targeted intervention must alter at least one causally relevant decision in the expected obligation family;
- unrelated sentinel state must not cause gratuitous architecture drift;
- removing a prerequisite must invalidate/replan downstream dependent transactions rather than merely rewrite prose;
- payoff urgency changes must affect selection/defer/weave when the obligation crosses the frozen pressure boundary;
- no post-cutoff leakage.

Exact batch size and aggregation threshold must be sealed before provider outputs after eligible-case inventory is known. Do not choose a threshold after seeing results.

- concrete action/opposition;
