## Research question
Did E6-R2's original formal qualification gates adequately measure **broadcast screenplay dramaturgy**, rather than only structural completeness, formatting, state continuity, semantic coverage, and internal-meta hygiene?

## Frozen audit dimensions
1. `DIALOGUE_AS_ACTION` — dialogue must perform a speech act toward another character rather than recite scene summaries, intent, state deltas, or character psychology.
2. `DIRECTION_DIALOGUE_SEPARATION` — stage direction must not paraphrase/explain the meaning of the adjacent dialogue, and dialogue must not merely verbalize the direction.
3. `DRAMATIC_AGENCY` — refusal, approval, confession, apology, trust change, and other decisive acts should normally be performed by the responsible character rather than reported by another character.
4. `CAUSAL_PROCESS_ONSCREEN` — major crisis resolution must show failure/attempt/new information-or-obstacle/choice/cost/consequence rather than jump from problem declaration to success declaration.
5. `SCENE_RHYTHM_VARIANCE` — cast size, utterance count, silence, action density and scene duration must vary according to scene function; a fixed scene template is disqualifying if systemic.
6. `CHARACTER_VOICE_DIFFERENTIATION` — recurring generic operating-language may not erase occupation, hierarchy, temperament and relationship-specific voice.
7. `SPATIAL_CONTINUITY` — scene location, co-presence versus remote communication, props and blocking must be shootable and internally legible.
8. `HUMAN_STAKE_VISIBILITY` — populations at risk may not exist only as counts/reports when their choices, resistance or consequences are central to the episode.
9. `CLIMAX_AND_ENDING_DISTINCTION` — climax and final image must differ materially from routine verification scenes.
10. `KOREAN_SURFACE_CORRECTNESS` — systematic particle/name insertion errors and production notation defects are tracked separately from dramaturgy but may independently block broadcast readiness.

## Audit decision rule
`CONSTRUCT_VALIDITY_FAIL` if **any one** of the following is supported by the full-script report with scene-level evidence:
- systemic scene-summary dialogue or third-person self-explanation;
- systemic fixed scene-template repetition materially suppressing scene rhythm/voice;
- decisive dramatic acts repeatedly reported by bystanders instead of enacted by responsible characters;
- major crisis resolutions repeatedly skip causal process;
- location/communication/prop continuity is repeatedly not shootable or ambiguous.

`CONSTRUCT_VALIDITY_HOLD` if the report is insufficient to establish any critical systemic defect.

The audit does **not** retroactively change the historical E6-R2 binary result under its original gates. Instead it changes the permissible **claim boundary** if construct validity fails.
